Built for every journey

A better place to wait.

ClimaStop combines protection from extreme weather with practical services that make public transport more comfortable, accessible and convenient.

☀

Solar panels

Roof-mounted solar panels help power lighting, displays and climate-control systems.

❄

Heating and cooling

Air conditioning and heating provide relief during hot summers and cold winter mornings.

🪑

Comfortable seating

Comfortable chairs give passengers a clean, sheltered place to sit while they wait.

🏠

Enclosed waiting room

An enclosed space protects passengers from rain, wind, wet benches and harsh weather.

♿

Accessible facilities

Female, male and unisex accessible toilets support a wider range of passenger needs.

🥤

Standing spaces

Areas where you don't have to sit down so that you have a choice.
